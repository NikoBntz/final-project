# Levinor Design System

## Brand & Style

Levinor is built on a **Corporate / Modern** design philosophy. It is engineered for professional, data-dense enterprise applications where reliability, clarity, and efficiency are paramount. The visual tone is structured and authoritative, utilizing a clean layout with controlled whitespace to minimize cognitive load during complex workflows.

## Colors

The color system is anchored by a deep professional blue (`#051559`) as the primary brand and interaction color, conveying stability and institutional trust. Supportive secondary (`#c35c0b`), tertiary (`#f5dcd5`), and neutral (`#ffffff`) tones complete a balanced light mode palette.

## Typography

The system uses **Bodoni Moda** for headlines to add editorial sophistication, **IBM Plex Serif** for body text to maintain exceptional legibility across dense reading contexts, and **JetBrains Mono** for labels and data displays to ensure crisp programmatic clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured **Fluid Grid** model is utilized, featuring standardized gutters and margins that adapt seamlessly across viewports, supported by a consistent spacing rhythm for dense enterprise forms and dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is handled through **Tonal layers and low-contrast outlines**. Heavy drop shadows are minimized in favor of structural background tinting and subtle borders to establish clear layering against the base canvas.

## Shapes

The shape language employs a **Rounded** profile (`roundedness: 2`), featuring a default 0.5rem corner radius for standard elements, balancing modern approachability with enterprise stability.

## Components

Components such as buttons, inputs, cards, chips, and tables are styled consistently with the established color, shape, and typography tokens to ensure a cohesive enterprise user experience.
